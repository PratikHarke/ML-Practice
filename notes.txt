ADABOOST

    -It is a boosting technique.
    -Decision Tree with a depth of 1 is called as Stump.
    -We create stumps and select the best one.
    -Calculate sum of Total Errors and Performance of stump.
    -Update the weights.
    -Normalized weights computation and assigning bias.
    -Select data points for next stump.
    -Final Prediction.
    
    Performance of stump - 1/n ln[1-TE/TE]

    For Updated Weights - 
    1] For correctly classified points
        = weight * e ^ -Performance of Stump

    2] For incorrectly classified points - 
        = weight * e ^ performance of stump
    

GRADIENT BOOSTING ALGORITHM
    
    Steps - 
    1] Create a Base Model.

    2] Compute Residuals and Errors.

    3] Construct a Decision Tree - Consider inputs xi and output R1.
